**Decision.** A rejected record holds until a new record supersedes it with a new reason.

**Why.** The new reason is what makes revisiting worthwhile, and a superseding record captures it. Time alone doesn't change why an idea was rejected.

**Rejected.** An optional `revisit:` date that `context` would mark once passed.
