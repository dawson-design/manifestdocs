**Decision.** Nodes have no dates. `mfd` shows only each issue's open or closed state.

**Why.** GitHub Projects already tracks dates, order and priority, and teams already plan there.

**Rejected.** `roadmap` and `ready` commands, and shipped dates worked out from closed issues.
