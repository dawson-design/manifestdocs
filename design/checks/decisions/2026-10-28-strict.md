**Decision.** `mfd check --strict` exits 1 when the tree has any findings, warnings included. The output is the same as without the flag.

**Why.** Warnings such as the node size limit keep context focused only if someone acts on them. A flag lets a team gate CI on them without making them errors for everyone.

**Rejected.** A `strict` setting in `manifestdocs.yaml`, which would also fail local runs and the plugin's after-edit hook. Turning warnings into errors.
